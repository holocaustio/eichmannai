Naftali Bar-Shalom, Rav-Pakad in the Israel Police and head of Section 1—Documentation of Bureau 06, testified that his duties encompassed sorting, authenticating, and preparing documents and testimonies for the investigative dossier. He recalled a vast archival pipeline, noting that the bulk of materials came from the German Foreign Ministry archives seized by the Allies, transported to Whaddon Hall near Oxford, processed through the German War Documents Project, and ferried as microfilms to Yad Vashem in Jerusalem before 98 volumes reached Bureau 06, where subordinates verified them and enlargements were prepared, each item stamped on the back with its microfilm number for traceability. His account underscored how such meticulous, transnational archival work—spanning the British Foreign Office, the American State Department, Yad Vashem, and Israeli authorities—provided the documentary foundation for the Eichmann trial and for the historical reconstruction of Nazi crimes and Jewish history.
