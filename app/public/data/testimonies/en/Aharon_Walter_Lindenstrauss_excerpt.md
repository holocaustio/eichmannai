Aharon Lindenstrauss, born in 1904 in Berlin, was a lawyer and Zionist who headed the Aliyah Department of the Palestine Office in Berlin from 1933 to 1939. He recounted the Kristallnacht aftermath, describing a wrecked Palestine office at Meinekestrasse 10 where only his room remained intact, and how he and fellow officials gathered to report on events and intervene as SS officers brought in keys to locate expiring Palestine certificates. He emphasized how emigration opportunities narrowed under relentless pressure, even as a delegation from Berlin’s Jewish institutions traveled to Vienna by Eichmann’s instruction, underscoring the bureaucratic obstruction and political coercion that shaped the fate of German Jewry.
